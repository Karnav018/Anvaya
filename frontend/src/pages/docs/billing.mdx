# Billing & plans

Anvaya has two plans during launch: **Free** and **Pro**.

## Plan comparison

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 forever | $29 / user / month |
| Generations per month | 10 | 500 |
| Team members | 1 | unlimited |
| Workspaces & roles | — | yes |
| Billing portal | — | yes |
| Support | community | priority |

## Generation quota

A *generation* is one click of the **Generate** button. Re-downloading a previously generated ZIP does not count.

The counter resets at the start of each calendar month, UTC. If you hit the cap on Free, you can still edit canvases — only generation is gated.

## Upgrading

From **Settings → Billing**, click **Upgrade to Pro**. You are redirected to Stripe Checkout. Once payment succeeds, your organisation flips to Pro immediately and the quota jumps to 500.

## Billing portal

On Pro you have access to Stripe's hosted billing portal. From **Settings → Billing → Manage**, you can:

- Update card details
- View and download invoices
- Cancel or downgrade
- Apply promo codes

## Downgrading

You can downgrade at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep Pro features until then.
